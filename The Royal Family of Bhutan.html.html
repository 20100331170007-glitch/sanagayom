The Royal Family of Bhutan

The Royal Family of Bhutan is an important and respected part of our country. The Royal Family belongs to the Wangchuck dynasty, which has guided Bhutan since the establishment of the monarchy in 1907. His Majesty King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck is the fifth King of Bhutan. He is admired for his humble nature, compassion, and dedication to the people. Her Majesty Queen Jetsun Pema is also greatly loved and respected for her work and involvement in the well-being of the people, especially children and young people. Their eldest son, Crown Prince Jigme Namgyel Wangchuck, is the heir to the throne, while their younger son is Prince Ugyen Wangchuck.The Royal Family has always placed the happiness and welfare of the Bhutanese people at the center of their service. They help promote education, environmental protection, cultural preservation, and national development. Members of the Royal Family often visit different parts of the country and meet people from all walks of life. Their close relationship with the people has created a strong sense of unity and trust. The Royal Family also represents Bhutanese values such as compassion, loyalty, respect, humility, and service to the nation. For many Bhutanese people, the Royal Family is a source of inspiration, pride, and national unity.
