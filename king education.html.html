King Education

King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck, the fifth King of Bhutan, received his education in Bhutan and abroad. He began his schooling in Bhutan and attended Yangchenphug High School in Thimphu. Later, he continued his education at Cushing Academy in Massachusetts, USA. He then attended Wheaton College, where he developed his knowledge of liberal arts and international affairs. He went on to study at Magdalen College, University of Oxford, in the United Kingdom, where his studies included politics, philosophy, economics, and international relations.In addition to his formal education, King Jigme Khesar received practical training in leadership, government, diplomacy, and public administration. As Crown Prince, he travelled throughout Bhutan and visited many countries, gaining experience in meeting people and understanding national and international affairs. His education and experiences helped prepare him for his responsibilities as King. He became the fifth King of Bhutan in 2006 and has continued to promote education, development, democracy, and the well-being of the people of Bhutan.
