MY KING

Bhutan has had five kings, known as the Druk Gyalpos or Dragon Kings, since the monarchy was established in 1907. The first King was Gongsa Ugyen Wangchuck, who united the country and established the Wangchuck dynasty. He was followed by King Jigme Wangchuck, who continued to maintain Bhutan's peace, independence, and traditions. The third King, King Jigme Dorji Wangchuck, is known as the Father of Modern Bhutan because he introduced many important developments in education, healthcare, roads, and administration. The fourth King, King Jigme Singye Wangchuck, introduced the philosophy of Gross National Happiness and guided Bhutan toward democracy and constitutional monarchy. The fifth and present King, King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck, continues to serve the people of Bhutan by promoting national development, education, environmental conservation, and the preservation of Bhutanese culture and traditions. The five Kings have played a very important role in building and protecting our country and have earned the deep respect and love of the Bhutanese people
